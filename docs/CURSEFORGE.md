**Your bank, always at hand.** PocketBank shows what is in your bank, bags and gear, for every character on your account, without going back to the bank. A modern alternative to BankItems (bank, bag and inventory viewer), which is no longer supported.

> **Coming soon.** First release in development. Features arrive in small, frequent updates.

## Planned
- Minimap button that opens a window with the contents of your bank, with the game's own look.
- Browse the bank of any of your characters, even when you are not logged in with them.
- Bags, equipment, item search and item tooltips showing what your other characters hold.
- Movable window that remembers its position, and an options panel.
- Guild bank.

English and Spanish.
